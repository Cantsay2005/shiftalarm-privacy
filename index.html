ShiftAlarm Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 18, 2026

ShiftAlarm is designed to help users manage rotating work schedules, overtime, shifts, and alarms. Privacy is built into the app by keeping app data on the user's device whenever possible.

1. Data Collection

ShiftAlarm does not collect personal information on developer-operated servers.

ShiftAlarm does not use:

  • Advertising networks
  • Analytics services
  • Tracking technologies
  • Third-party data brokers
  • User accounts
  • Developer-operated cloud storage
  • Developer-operated telemetry or crash-reporting services

Based on the current implementation, information stored and processed by ShiftAlarm remains on the user's device.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

ShiftAlarm may store the following information locally:

  • Work schedules
  • Shift times and rotation patterns
  • Overtime entries and overtime presets
  • Time-off dates, reasons, and notes
  • Alarm timing preferences
  • Alarm sound selections
  • Snooze preferences
  • Appearance and display preferences
  • Other app settings
  • Alarm identifiers and scheduling information required to manage alarms

This information is stored locally using Apple platform storage technologies such as SwiftData and UserDefaults.

3. Alarm Sounds and Imported Audio

ShiftAlarm allows users to select or import compatible audio for use as alarm sounds.

Depending on the feature used, ShiftAlarm may locally process:

  • Audio files selected through the Files interface
  • Compatible music selected from the user's music library
  • Song title and artist information
  • Locally generated alarm tones

When compatible audio is imported, ShiftAlarm may create a short local audio copy and processed volume variants for use with Apple's AlarmKit.

These files are stored locally within ShiftAlarm's application storage, including the app's Library/Sounds directory.

ShiftAlarm does not upload imported music or audio to developer-operated servers.

Protected Apple Music subscription content and other inaccessible media may not be usable as alarm sounds.

4. Music Library Access

ShiftAlarm may request access to the user's music library so the user can choose compatible music for alarm sounds.

Music-library access is used only for features initiated by the user. Users can manage or revoke this permission through iOS Settings.

5. AlarmKit

ShiftAlarm uses Apple's AlarmKit framework to schedule alarms requested by the user.

AlarmKit may store and present alarm-related information through Apple-managed system services. ShiftAlarm does not operate a remote server for AlarmKit data.

6. Live Activities

ShiftAlarm may use Apple's Live Activities system to display alarm-related information on supported devices.

Live Activity presentation is handled through Apple platform technologies.

7. Files Access

ShiftAlarm may allow users to select an audio file using Apple's document picker.

The app receives access only to files selected by the user. ShiftAlarm does not scan or access unrelated files on the device.

8. Data Transmission

ShiftAlarm does not intentionally transmit schedules, overtime information, notes, settings, imported audio, or other app data to the developer.

Certain operating-system services may communicate with Apple or with a file-storage provider selected by the user. Examples may include:

  • Device backups
  • Apple system services
  • Cloud-based file providers used through the Files app
  • Apple-managed diagnostics or operating-system services

These services are controlled by Apple or the applicable third-party provider and are not developer-operated data collection by ShiftAlarm.

9. Data Retention

App data remains stored on the device until it is changed or removed by the user, removed by an app feature, or removed when the app and its associated local data are deleted.

Some generated or imported alarm-sound files may remain stored locally while they are needed by existing alarms.

Resetting app preferences may not remove schedules, imported alarm sounds, or all other stored data.

10. Deleting Your Data

ShiftAlarm currently does not maintain user accounts or developer-operated servers, so there is no remote user account or server-side data profile to delete.

Users may remove locally stored information using available app controls.

Deleting ShiftAlarm from the device removes the app's locally stored application data according to Apple's normal app-deletion behavior.

Device backups are controlled separately by Apple and should be managed through Apple settings if the user also wants backed-up copies removed.

11. Permissions

ShiftAlarm may request permission for:

  • Alarm scheduling
  • Music-library access

Users can manage or revoke supported permissions through iOS Settings. Revoking a permission may prevent related ShiftAlarm features from working.

12. Tracking and Advertising

ShiftAlarm does not track users across apps or websites.

ShiftAlarm does not display third-party advertising and does not sell personal information.

13. Children's Privacy

ShiftAlarm does not knowingly collect personal information from children through developer-operated servers.

Because ShiftAlarm does not operate user accounts or remote data collection, the developer does not knowingly maintain personal profiles of child users.

14. Security

ShiftAlarm relies on Apple's application sandbox and platform security features to protect locally stored application data.

Users are responsible for protecting access to their device and Apple account.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if ShiftAlarm's features or data practices change.

If future versions add features such as analytics, accounts, cloud synchronization, advertising, or developer-operated network services, this policy and the app's App Store privacy disclosures will be updated accordingly.
