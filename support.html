Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set up my work schedule?

Open OddO’clock and go to Schedule to configure your shifts and repeating work rotation.

How do I manage alarms?

Open the Alarms section to review your scheduled alarms. Alarm settings can be adjusted to match your work schedule.

Can I add overtime?

Yes. OddO’clock supports overtime scheduling independently of your regular work rotation.

Why aren't my alarms appearing?

Check that the relevant shift or overtime entry is enabled. Also review alarm permissions and notification settings on your iPhone.
